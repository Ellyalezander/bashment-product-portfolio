# Bashment User Personas

## 1. Patrons

### Overview

People looking for nightlife and cultural experiences.

### Primary Needs

- Discover relevant events
- Understand what is happening nearby
- Evaluate event activity
- Find experiences that match their interests
- Make better decisions about where to go

### Product Value

Bashment brings event discovery and activity signals into one experience, helping patrons discover and evaluate nightlife and cultural experiences.

---

## 2. Promoters

### Overview

Individuals or organizations responsible for organizing and promoting events.

### Primary Needs

- Create and manage events
- Reach potential attendees
- Understand event activity
- Monitor engagement
- Gain visibility into event performance
- Identify opportunities to improve event outcomes

### Product Value

Bashment provides promoters with tools and intelligence designed to improve visibility into their events and help inform promotional decisions.

---

## 3. Venues

### Overview

Businesses that host nightlife and cultural events.

### Primary Needs

- Understand event activity
- Monitor attendance-related signals
- Evaluate event performance
- Understand audience engagement
- Identify opportunities to improve venue performance

### Product Value

Bashment is designed to provide venues with data and intelligence that can support better operational and event-related decisions.

---

## Shared Intelligence Opportunity

All three user groups contribute to and benefit from the Bashment intelligence ecosystem.

**PATRONS → ACTIVITY → DATA → INTELLIGENCE → PROMOTERS & VENUES**

As the platform grows, increased activity and completed-event data can improve the quality of signals, forecasts, and recommendations available across the ecosystem.

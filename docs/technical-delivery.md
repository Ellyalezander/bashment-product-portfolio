# Bashment Technical Delivery

## Role

As Founder & Product Lead, I manage the product and technical delivery lifecycle for Bashment.

The role combines product strategy, requirements, technical coordination, implementation oversight, testing, deployment, and continuous improvement.

---

## Product & Technical Responsibilities

### Product Strategy

- Define product objectives
- Translate business problems into product requirements
- Prioritize product capabilities
- Maintain product direction

### Requirements

- Define functional requirements
- Document user needs
- Develop user stories
- Establish acceptance criteria
- Identify dependencies and constraints

### Technical Coordination

- Coordinate technical implementation
- Evaluate platform and integration requirements
- Work across APIs and third-party services
- Support database and data requirements
- Coordinate product changes through development and testing

## Development Lifecycle

Bashment follows an iterative delivery cycle:

**REQUIRE → DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → DEPLOY → MEASURE → IMPROVE**

### REQUIRE

Define the user need, business objective, requirements, and acceptance criteria.

### DESIGN

Determine the appropriate product experience and technical approach.

### BUILD

Implement the required functionality.

### TEST

Validate functionality, identify issues, and collect beta feedback.

### DEPLOY

Release validated functionality into the appropriate product environment.

### MEASURE

Evaluate usage, feedback, data quality, and product outcomes.

### IMPROVE

Use findings to prioritize subsequent product changes.

## Technical Delivery Principles

### User-Centered

Technical decisions should support a clearly identified user or business need.

### Iterative

Build and validate incrementally rather than attempting to define the entire product upfront.

### Measurable

Product capabilities should produce measurable outcomes wherever practical.

### Explainable

Intelligence features should provide understandable reasoning and avoid unnecessary black-box outputs.

### Scalable

Technical decisions should support future product growth without prematurely over-engineering the MVP.

### Evidence-Driven

Beta behavior, feedback, and completed-event outcomes should inform future product decisions.
